Description

This recipe describes the fastest sponge cake known to mankind.

Ingredients

For the spongecake:

  • Two eggs
  • 100g of flour
  • 100g of sugar
  • 100g of melted butter
  • 1 tsp of baking powder
  • 2 Passion fruits, get the wrinkly ones

For the coulis:

  • 175g of red berries
  • 50g of red sugar

Steps

  1. In a medium sized bowl melt the butter in the microwave, takes approx. 1 minute
  2. Cut the passion fruits into halves and scoop the contents into the bowl with the melted butter
  3. Add in the rest of ingredients for the spongecake, and whisk rigorously with a weapon of your choice untill there are no lumps.
  4. Butter and sugar a few ramekins or any device that is microwave safe
  5. Fill the spongecake devices about halfway, and cover each of them with cling film to form a temporary lid
  6. For the coulis combine the berries and sugar in a small pot and put on the stove over high heat, untill the berries have released their natural liquids. Set aside.
  7. Microwave the spongecakes for about 40-45 seconds each
  8. Invert the spongecake onto a plate, and top with the berry coulis and optionally whipped cream.
