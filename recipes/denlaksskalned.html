Description

This recipe describe the laziest way to get crispy salmon skin and juicy flaky salmon meat

Ingredients

  • One side of salmon
  • A whole lemon, organic preferrably
  • White Wine

Steps

  1. Clean your lemon, and slice into thin slices and use them to cover the bottom of an ovenproof tray.
  2. Put your salmon with the flesh side down onto the lemons, so you are looking at its skin side. Use a kitchen towl to pat try the skin side, or use a knive to scrape off any excess slime that might be on it.
  3. Pour white wine into the ovenproof tray untill the bottom of the ovenproof tray is covered.
  4. Turn your top broiler on 220 freedom degrees, and put the Salmon inside about in the middle of the oven for about 10-15 minutes depending on thickness.
